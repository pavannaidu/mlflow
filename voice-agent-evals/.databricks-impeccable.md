## Design Context

### Surface type
Databricks App (FastAPI + React, Vite), `app/`.

### Audience
Field demo: a Databricks SA presents it to customer teams evaluating voice agents.

### Theme
Light.

### Use case
Compare voice-agent candidates stage by stage (hearing, reasoning, speaking), play the audio next
to the MLflow trace text, and rate spoken answers.

### Brand (locked)
- Palette: Lava 600 #FF3621 (10% accent), Navy 900 #0B2026, Light #F9F7F4
- Typography: DM Sans 400/500/700 + DM Mono (self-hosted in `app/frontend/public/fonts`)
- Components: custom, matching Du Bois patterns (Du Bois isn't available outside Databricks)
- Icons: Lucide
- Voice: Databricks editorial-plain (no superlatives, plain statements)

### Additional constraints
- Mark the data "Illustrative data" once, in the header.
- Attribution colors (validated with the dataviz palette checker on white): pass #00A972,
  ASR-caused #4462C9, reasoning #FF7033, flaky neutral #CBD5E0. Orange is below 3:1 contrast,
  so every segment carries a visible count and the legend is always shown.
